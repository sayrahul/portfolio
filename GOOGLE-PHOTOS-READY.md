## 🔧 **Troubleshooting:**

### **Images not loading?**

1. **Check Console (F12):**
   - Should see: `"✅ Loaded 30 projects"`
   - If you see errors, let me know

2. **Check Album Privacy:**
   - Album must be set to "Anyone with link"
   - Open album in incognito - should work without login

3. **Try Hard Refresh:**
   - Press `Ctrl + Shift + R` (Windows)
   - Or `Cmd + Shift + R` (Mac)

### **Images loading slowly?**

- Normal! Google Photos serves high-quality images
- They'll load faster after first visit (cached)
